# My Desktop Garden 

"Some flowers are meant to bloom for only a season."

Immerse yourself in the late 90s with My Desktop Garden. In this game, you chat with your special someone in the span of three days. After each conversation, you must water your plants from your desktop garden to receive your inspirational quote of the day! 

"Flowers are not supposed to last. They are a natural reminder to stay in the present and appreciate the connections we have."
